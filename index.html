Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior AI/ML experience to attend?

Not at all. DevConf 2026 has tracks for every level — beginner-friendly workshops as well as deep technical talks for experienced engineers.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes, all sessions are recorded. Standard ticket holders get 30 days of access, while Pro ticket holders get lifetime access.

Is there a refund policy?

Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the event. After that, tickets can be transferred to another attendee.

Can my company get a group discount?

Yes, teams of 5 or more get a discount. Reach out through the Contact Us button for a custom quote.
